小休闹钟隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

一句话：小休闹钟不收集、不上传你的任何个人信息。没有帐号，没有广告，没有统计或其他第三方 SDK。你的闹钟、请假和设置只保存在你的手机上。

1. 保存在手机上的数据

  • 你填的内容：闹钟、请假、在日历上改过的日子、订阅的日历地址、各项设置。
  • 下载的公开数据：公共假期、节日和订阅日历的日程，缓存在手机上，减少联网。
  • 只在本机：以上数据都只存在 App 自己的存储里，我们（开发者）收不到。卸载 App 就会删除。
  • 系统备份：如果你使用手机系统的备份与恢复功能，这些数据会随系统备份一起保存，由系统和你选择的备份位置管理。

2. 联网访问了哪些地方

App 只为获取公开数据而联网，请求里只有地区、年份或日历地址，不包含你的个人信息。下面这些服务会像访问任何网站一样看到你的 IP 地址等网络信息，并按它们各自的隐私政策处理：

  • 中国大陆的节假日和调休：raw.githubusercontent.com、cdn.jsdelivr.net（开源项目 holiday-cn 的数据），以及 registry.npmmirror.com（开源项目 chinese-days 的数据）。App 里还内置了一份，联网取不到时使用。
  • 其他国家和地区的公共假期：date.nager.at（Nager.Date）、calendar.google.com（Google 公开的节假日日历）。
  • 你订阅的日历：你在 App 里订阅的日历地址，例如内置目录里的赛程日历（ics.fixtur.es、files-f1.motorsportcalendars.com、www.smartcalendars.ai 等），或你自己填写的地址。
  • 检测能否访问 Google：打开日历页时访问一次 www.google.com/generate_204（一个空页面，没连上再试一次），用来决定点开日程时用哪个搜索引擎，以及日历订阅目录里显示哪些日历：访问不了 Google 时，目录不显示只能从 Google 获取的日历，订阅的节假日改用上面的公共假期数据。月相在手机上计算，不联网。

3. 打开网页

  • 点订阅日历里的日程：会用手机浏览器打开这条日程的网页；没有网页的，用搜索引擎（Google、Bing 或百度，可在设置里选）搜索它的标题。这只在你点击时发生。
  • 系统日历里的日程不会：不会被拿去搜索，也不会发到任何地方。

4. 权限

  • 读取日历（读取日历、读取全部日历）：只在你在设置里开启「显示系统日历里的日程」时申请，用来把你在系统日历里的日程显示在小休闹钟的月历和当天详情里。只在本机读取和显示，不修改、不上传。可以随时在 App 设置里关掉显示，或到手机「设置 › 应用和元服务 › 小休闹钟 › 权限」里收回。
  • 通知、代理提醒：把闹钟交给系统按时响铃、显示提醒通知。
  • 振动：拖动操作时的触感反馈。
  • 网络：获取第 2 节列出的公开数据。

5. 同意、撤回和删除数据

  • 第一次打开时请你同意本政策：同意之前 App 不联网，也不申请任何权限；不同意就无法使用，可以直接退出。
  • 随时可以撤回：在 App 的「设置 › 隐私 › 撤回同意」里撤回，App 会退出，下次打开要重新同意。
  • 删除数据：所有数据只在你的手机上，卸载 App 即全部删除，我们这边没有任何你的数据需要删除。

6. 儿童

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

7. 政策更新

App 的功能变化涉及数据处理时会更新本政策，并修改上面的生效日期；有实质变化时，App 会在打开时请你重新同意。

8. 联系我们

对本政策有任何问题，请发邮件到 a1002641738@gmail.com。

Holiday Alarm Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short: Holiday Alarm does not collect or upload any of your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or other third-party SDKs. Your alarms, leave and settings stay on your phone.

1. Data stored on your phone

  • What you enter: alarms, leave, days you changed on the calendar, calendar subscription addresses and settings.
  • Public data downloaded: public holidays, festivals and events of subscribed calendars, cached on your phone to reduce network use.
  • On your phone only: all of this stays in the app's own storage and is never sent to us, the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • System backup: if you use your phone's backup and restore feature, this data is included in the system backup and handled by the system and the backup location you choose.

2. Where the app connects

The app goes online only to fetch public data. Requests contain only a region, a year or a calendar address, never your personal information. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and other standard network information, and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Public holidays and make-up workdays in mainland China: raw.githubusercontent.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net (data from the open-source project holiday-cn) and registry.npmmirror.com (data from the open-source project chinese-days). A copy is also built into the app for when these cannot be reached.
  • Public holidays in other countries and regions: date.nager.at (Nager.Date) and calendar.google.com (Google's public holiday calendars).
  • Calendars you subscribe to: the addresses of calendars you subscribe to in the app, such as the sports calendars in the built-in catalog (ics.fixtur.es, files-f1.motorsportcalendars.com, www.smartcalendars.ai, etc.) or addresses you enter yourself.
  • Checking whether Google is reachable: when the calendar page opens, the app loads www.google.com/generate_204 (an empty page) once, trying once more if it fails, to decide which search engine to use when you open an event, and which calendars the subscription catalog shows: if Google is unreachable, the catalog hides calendars only available from Google, and subscribed holidays use the public holiday data above instead. Moon phases are calculated on your phone, without going online.

3. Opening web pages

  • Tapping an event of a subscribed calendar: opens the event's web page in your browser; if it has none, searches its title with a search engine (Google, Bing or Baidu, selectable in settings). This happens only when you tap.
  • Events from your system calendar never do: they are never searched or sent anywhere.

4. Permissions

  • Calendar (read calendar, read all calendars): requested only when you turn on "Show events from the system calendar" in settings, to show the events in your system calendar on Holiday Alarm's month view and day details. Events are read and shown on your phone only, never changed or uploaded. You can turn the display off in the app's settings at any time, or revoke the permission in phone Settings › Apps & services › Holiday Alarm › Permissions.
  • Notifications and agent reminders: let the system ring your alarms on time and show the reminder notification.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback while dragging.
  • Internet: to fetch the public data listed in section 2.

5. Consent, withdrawal and deleting data

  • You are asked to agree to this policy the first time you open the app: until you agree, the app does not go online or request any permission. If you disagree, you cannot use the app and can simply close it.
  • Withdraw at any time: in the app's Settings › Privacy › Withdraw consent. The app closes, and you will be asked to agree again next time.
  • Deleting data: all data stays on your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

6. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If a change to the app affects how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above changed. For material changes, the app will ask you to agree again when you open it.
